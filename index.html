Sell your Gilbert or Phoenix-area house as-is

Bridge Investments Group buys residential houses for its own account. If the property is in Gilbert or the rest of the Phoenix metro and it is a fit, we can make a cash offer. You decide whether to accept it.

  • We buy as-is. You do not have to repair the house first.
  • We are the buyer, not a listing agent, so we do not charge you a listing commission.
  • An offer is not guaranteed. Nothing on this site obligates you to sell.

How a cash sale with us works

Three steps. You can stop at any point before a written agreement is signed.

Step 1

Send the property details

Use the form with your name and email. A mobile number and the property address help us respond, but texting is optional.

Step 2

We review what you sent

If we can buy the house, we follow up by phone, email, or text if you opted in. We do not quote a price on this website.

Step 3

Close only if you agree in writing

A purchase happens only after both sides sign a written agreement. You choose whether to move forward and on what timeline you can accept.
