Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026. Last updated: September 30, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Bridge Investments Group LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”), doing business as Bridge Investments Group, handles information when you use this website or contact us about selling a house. Contact: Alec Thomas, 1509 W Lobster Trap Drive, Gilbert, AZ 85233, (815) 714-3499, arinrealfliphq@gmail.com.

Who we are

Bridge Investments Group LLC, doing business as Bridge Investments Group, buys residential houses as-is in Gilbert, Arizona and the Phoenix metro. This website is for homeowners considering a sale to us. We do not operate a consumer-lending or securities business through this site, and we do not operate a mobile app.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to give us, including:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number, if you enter one
  • Property address, if you enter one
  • Any message you type, and records of calls or emails you send us
  • Whether you checked the optional text-message consent box

If you only browse the site, the host may log ordinary technical data such as IP address, browser type, and the page requested. We do not use that log data to send marketing texts.

Where phone numbers come from

Mobile numbers used for this texting program are collected only when you type a number into the cash-offer form on this website and submit it. We do not buy, rent, scrape, or upload third-party phone lists for texting.

No sharing of mobile numbers for marketing

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or consent records with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing. A service provider that sends texts or hosts the site may process a number only to perform that service for us, and not to market its own products. We do not transfer your consent so that another company can text you.

How we use information

  • To review a house you asked us to consider buying
  • To respond by phone or email
  • To send text messages only if you provided a mobile number and checked the consent box
  • To schedule a conversation or visit you agree to
  • To keep ordinary records if a purchase moves forward

Text messages

If you opt in, we may send recurring automated informational and marketing text messages about cash home-offer updates, scheduling, and follow-up on the request you submitted. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase and is not required to receive an offer. Program details are in our SMS Terms and Terms.

Other sharing

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights in a dispute about a transaction you asked us to consider. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies

These pages are static. They do not set advertising cookies and they do not include third-party ad pixels. A host may keep server logs needed to deliver the pages.

Retention

We keep inquiry records long enough to respond and to keep ordinary business records. You may ask us to delete an inquiry, subject to records we must keep by law.

Your choices

  • Leave the SMS box unchecked if you do not want texts. You can still call or email.
  • Reply STOP to a Bridge Investments Group text to opt out.
  • Email arinrealfliphq@gmail.com to ask for access, a correction, or deletion of an inquiry.

Children

This site is for adults dealing with a house sale. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and post the new version at privacy.html on this site.
